## 1. Brand Decision

**Primary tagline: "DatIQ — Intelligence, Connected."**
**Enterprise descriptor:** *Turn web, social, and market signals into automated revenue workflows.*
**A/B challenger:** "Web Intelligence, Delivered." (Claude's top-scored candidate; run as hero variant B for 30 days).
**Retired:** "Intelligence from every URL" (anchors to an input, not an outcome — Gemini critique, unanimously accepted); "Intelligence from Web" (current interim line — generic, no value driver).

Why "Intelligence, Connected." won (4–1): two-word memorability · pillar-proof (covers extraction, social, brand, AI visibility, agents — nothing to outgrow) · "Connected" claims the connector/orchestration moat no competitor names · reads equally well as consumer-simple and enterprise-serious · pairs cleanly with the pillar architecture already visible on-site ("Pillar 0: Web Intelligence Core").

**Voice:** confident, concrete, zero scraper-jargon on the homepage ("extract intelligence," never "scrape"). Persona depth lives on the five landing routes (/lead-generation, /competitor-pricing, /seo-audit, /developers, /ai-visibility), all funneling into the same URL box.

## 3. Three Visual Hero Concepts

### Concept A — "The Dashboard Reveal" ⭐ Recommended · A/B priority 1 (conversion)
The product is the hero. A partially transparent DatIQ dashboard floats right of the text at slight 3-D perspective (rotateX 5°, rotateY −3°, soft drop shadow), showing three interlocking panels: extraction result (left), sentiment-colored mention feed with an intent badge glowing on one card (center), CRM sync panel with HubSpot/Apollo logos and green checks (right).
**Layout:** F-pattern. Headline upper-left, 48–56px bold Inter; subhead 18px; CTAs lower-left; dashboard occupies right 55% of viewport. Background #fafaf8 with 2%-opacity dot grid (light default; **a dark-mode variant ships day one** — the executive dark aesthetic tests well with the RevOps/CMO segment and matches Concept C's enterprise energy).
**Mobile:** dashboard scales and stacks below text.
**Why:** visitors see themselves using the product before signing up; strongest for the Start-Free goal. Effort: medium (Figma mock, no animation dependency).

### Concept B — "The Data Constellation" · A/B priority 3 (brand/retargeting)
Abstract network of 15–20 pulsing nodes (CSS scale oscillation 0.95↔1.05 / 3s) labeled URL, Tweet, Review, CRM Contact, Price, Sentiment, **AI Answer** — lines converging into a bright central DatIQ node. Accent color on core nodes, muted periphery; constellation fades left-to-right (full opacity near text → 30% at edge). White background; copy center-left.
**Mobile:** reduce to 8–10 nodes.
**Why:** brand-forward "Intelligence, Connected." literalized; ideal for retargeting audiences who already know the product. Risk: abstract — weakest for cold-traffic comprehension. Effort: low (SVG/CSS).

### Concept C — "The Before/After Split" · A/B priority 2 (cold-traffic education)
Split screen. Left ("before"): desaturated, 1px-blurred collage of chaos — raw browser text, overlapping tweet bubbles, a messy spreadsheet, half-empty CRM fields. Right ("after"): crisp, colorful order — clean data table, sentiment trend chart, enriched CRM card, an alert toast reading *"Competitor pricing changed 12 min ago."* Bold vertical divider with the DatIQ mark centered on it — the platform as the transformative bridge. Tagline spans full width above; subhead + CTAs below.
**Mobile:** halves stack vertically, "after" on top.
**Why:** strongest problem→solution story for first-time visitors; the alert toast smuggles the differentiator into the visual. Effort: high (dual illustration + responsive split).

### A/B & rollout plan
Ship A as default at rebrand launch (Sprint 0.2, task P0-19). Build C during Sprint 1.1 idle design capacity; run A-vs-C on cold traffic for 30 days post-Product Hunt. B reserved for retargeting campaigns and the /developers route (constellation reads well to technical audiences). Tagline test runs orthogonally: "Intelligence, Connected." vs "Web Intelligence, Delivered." at 50/50 within the winning visual. Decision metric: signup conversion (primary), first-extraction activation (guardrail — a hero that converts but mis-sets expectations will show up here).
