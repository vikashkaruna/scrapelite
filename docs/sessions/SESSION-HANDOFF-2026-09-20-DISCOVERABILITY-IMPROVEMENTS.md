## 2. What Was Accomplished

Addressed the 8 audit findings identified by the Discoverability audit engine:

### 1. Head Signals Preservation (`fetchLayer.js`) — TA-12, TA-06, TA-15, EA-01, Mobile Parity
- **Finding 1 & 5 (No mobile parity / TA-12 No viewport declaration)**: `fetchLayer.js` was choosing `primaryHtml = renderedHtml` whenever headless providers (Firecrawl / Spider) returned a document matching `/<html[\s>]/i`. However, headless scrapers often return DOM bodies wrapped in minimal `<html><body>...</body></html>` tags without preserving the full `<head>` (`<meta name="viewport">`, `<link rel="canonical">`, and `<script type="application/ld+json">`).
- **Solution**: Implemented `preserveHeadSignals(primary, raw)` in `netlify/functions/lib/audit/fetchLayer.js`. When `renderedHtml` is missing `<meta name="viewport">`, `<link rel="canonical">`, or `<script type="application/ld+json">`, the head signals from `rawHtml` are spliced into `primaryHtml`.
- **Result**: Viewport tag is preserved (`signals.mobile_parity = 100`, no `MOBILE_PARITY_MISSING` penalty, no `TA-12` issue). Canonical links (TA-06) and JSON-LD schemas (TA-15, EA-01) are consistently maintained regardless of which scraping engine in the fallback chain responds.

### 2. Organization Schema & Profile Parity (`Footer.jsx`, `index.html`) — EA-01, EA-03
- **Finding 4 (EA-03 No sameAs links to official profiles)**: `Footer.jsx` used generic links (`https://linkedin.com` and `https://twitter.com`), which differed from the `Organization` schema's canonical profile URLs.
- **Solution**:
  - Updated `Footer.jsx` social links to:
    - LinkedIn: `https://www.linkedin.com/company/datiq`
    - Twitter / X: `https://twitter.com/DatIQApp`
  - In `index.html` (and prerendered pages):
    - Removed `https://github.com/vikashkaruna/scrapelite` from `sameAs`.
    - Replaced `https://twitter.com/datiq_app` with `https://twitter.com/DatIQApp`.
    - Updated `<meta name="twitter:site" content="@DatIQApp" />`.
    - Exact `sameAs` array: `["https://www.linkedin.com/company/datiq", "https://twitter.com/DatIQApp", "https://www.linkedin.com/company/axiom-minds/"]`.

### 3. Structured Data in Static & Help Center Pages — TA-15, EA-01
- **Finding 3 & 7 (No Organization schema / No structured data)**: The 25 static pages generated by `docs/build-help.mjs` and `public/faq/index.html` lacked JSON-LD blocks.
- **Solution**:
  - In `docs/build-help.mjs`, updated the `shell()` generator to emit OpenGraph tags, Twitter card meta, and JSON-LD `@graph` containing `Organization` (with complete `sameAs`), `TechArticle`, and `BreadcrumbList`.
  - Regenerated all 25 pages in `public/help/`.
  - Added `Organization` JSON-LD schema to `public/faq/index.html`.

### 4. Industry Landing Pages — AC-11
- **Finding 8 (AC-11 The site publishes no industry pages)**: The sitemap had zero URLs matching industry patterns (`/for-(finance|healthcare|retail|legal)`).
- **Solution**:

    - `/for-finance` (Financial Services & FinTech)
    - `/for-healthcare` (Healthcare & Life Sciences)
    - `/for-retail` (E-Commerce & Retail)
    - `/for-legal` (Legal & Compliance)
  - Updated `src/pages/ProgrammaticRoute.jsx` to render `route.kind === "industry"` hero kicker and section headers.
  - Registered routes in `src/App.jsx`, added SEO segment names in `src/lib/pageSeo.js`, and registered in `REACT_OWNED` in `scripts/site-routes.mjs`.
  - Prerendered to `public/for-finance/index.html`, `public/for-healthcare/index.html`, `public/for-retail/index.html`, and `public/for-legal/index.html`.
  - Added routes to `public/sitemap.xml` (sitemap now contains 64 URLs).

### 5. Answer Engine Citation References — EA-08
- **Finding 2 (EA-08 Answer engines never cite this domain)**:
  - Updated `public/llms.txt` and `public/llms-full.txt` to list the 4 industry pages and official Twitter profile (`https://twitter.com/DatIQApp`).

### 6. Tooling & Test Harness Enhancements
- **Fixed `columnsFrom` in `scripts/verify-workflows-e2e.mjs`**: When PostgREST queries request embedded relations with commas (e.g. `select="*, watchlist_targets(id, domain)"`), splitting naively on comma left trailing expressions (e.g. `domain)`) which caused Postgres syntax errors. Updated `columnsFrom` to strip embedded relation expressions before splitting on comma.
- **Fixed `scripts/pre-push.sh`**:
  - Added automatic Node 24 selection via nvm if present so subshells do not default to Homebrew Node v26.
  - Wrapped `tput` invocations so failure in non-color terminals does not trip `set -e`.
