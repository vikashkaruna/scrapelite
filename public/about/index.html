The product, organised

Built on a named foundation. Pillar 0 ships today.

DatIQ is structured as a stack of pillars. Pillar 0 — Web Intelligence (Core) is the single, batch, and scheduled URL-extraction engine that every other capability in the product is built on. It is the proven foundation; the rest of the platform is what you can do once you have it.

Pillar 0 Live
Web Intelligence (Core)

The proven single, batch, and scheduled URL-extraction engine that the whole platform is built on. Headings, links, contacts, pricing, custom fields, AI summaries, CSV/PDF export, domain mapping — every DatIQ capability you use today runs on Pillar 0.

Pillar 1 Live
Enrichment & Insight

AI summaries, lead scoring, contact enrichment, social-link discovery, company-mission extraction, and content generation (SEO outlines, competitor briefs, social posts) — all running on the structured data Pillar 0 returns.

Pillar 2 Live
Distribution & Workflow

CSV / PDF / Google Sheets export, scheduled monitoring with alerts, email delivery, webhook push, and CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce). The intelligence leaves the app on your terms.

Pillar 3 Roadmap
Workspace & Collaboration

Shared workspaces, role-based access, audit trails, and team-level usage controls — for agencies, research teams, and revenue ops running DatIQ at scale.

Pillar 4 Roadmap
Intelligence Mesh (API & Integrations)

A first-class REST + webhook API, native integrations (Zapier, Make, n8n, HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, Airtable), and an SDK so DatIQ's intelligence can be embedded anywhere.
