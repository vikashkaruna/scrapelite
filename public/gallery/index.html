The interactive gallery lives in the app

Public extractions are user-driven — anyone with a DatIQ account can hit the Share button on any extraction to publish it at datiq.app/p/<slug>. The interactive gallery aggregates every shared extraction your browser has seen, plus a curated set of seed reports, and lets you filter by tag, intent, and persona.

Example public report formats

When you share an extraction from the app, it becomes a public read-only report. Each report shows:

  • Page title, URL, and a plain-language AI summary at the top
  • Headings H1–H6 in document order, with click-to-anchor
  • Internal and external links, filterable and AI-categorized
  • Enrichment tabs: contacts, social, mission, pricing, custom fields
  • "Open in DatIQ" CTA for recipients who want to extract their own data
