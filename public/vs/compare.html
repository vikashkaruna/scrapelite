Who should use what?

Choose DatIQ if…

You want speed and simplicity

You need structured data from any URL without writing code. You want AI summaries, batch mode, and CSV/PDF export in one tool that works in 30 seconds. Especially strong for Indian teams (INR billing, DPDP compliance).

Choose Browse.ai if…

You need visual robot automation

You want to record browser interactions visually and replay them on a schedule. Best for structured table extraction and monitoring with a point-and-click robot interface.

Choose Clay if…

You're a sales team doing GTM enrichment

You work primarily with contacts and companies and want deep multi-source enrichment (LinkedIn, Clearbit, Hunter) in a spreadsheet-like CRM workspace. Best for SDR workflows.

Choose Apify if…

You're a developer needing full control

You want a developer-grade platform with custom actors, full headless Chrome, proxy rotation, and programmatic scheduling. Requires coding but offers maximum flexibility.
