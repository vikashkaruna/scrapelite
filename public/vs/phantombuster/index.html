Who should use what?

Choose DatIQ if…

You need broad web extraction + AI

You want to extract structured data from any web page — competitor sites, product pages, directories, SaaS landing pages — and enrich it with AI summaries, generate content, and export to CSV/PDF. Strong fit for marketing, SEO, and research teams.

Choose PhantomBuster if…

You're running LinkedIn outreach

Your primary need is scraping LinkedIn profiles, exporting connection lists, or automating outreach sequences on LinkedIn/Twitter. PhantomBuster's pre-built phantoms are best-in-class for social selling and SDR automation workflows.
