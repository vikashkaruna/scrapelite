Who should use what?

Choose DatIQ if…

You need no-code extraction with enrichment baked in

You want structured data from any URL in under 30 seconds, without writing code, and you value the built-in contacts / leadership / pricing / social enrichment tabs. DatIQ is ideal for sales, marketing, research, and SEO teams that want results, not infrastructure.

Choose Firecrawl if…

You're a developer building a RAG or LLM pipeline

You want full programmatic control over every request, you need raw markdown / JSON at scale (millions of pages a month), and you live in your IDE rather than a web app. Firecrawl's API and SDKs are built for that exact shape of work.
