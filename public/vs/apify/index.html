Who should use what?

Choose DatIQ if…

You need fast, AI-enabled extraction

You want to extract structured data from any URL in under 30 seconds, without writing a single line of code. DatIQ's AI-powered approach, batch mode, and built-in enrichment tabs are ideal for marketing, sales, and research teams.

Choose Apify if…

You're a developer who needs full control

You need to build custom scrapers with proxy rotation, handle CAPTCHAs, scrape at massive scale, or integrate deeply via API. Apify gives developers maximum power — but requires coding and a much steeper learning curve.
