DatIQ · Integrations

Move data into the tools your team already uses

DatIQ ships with CSV, PDF, and Google Sheets export on every plan, plus webhooks and one-click pushes to Airtable and Notion. Coming-soon items are on the public roadmap.

Available

CSV Export

Download any extraction or dashboard selection as a clean, structured CSV file. Works on all plans including Free.

Available

PDF Export

Generate a formatted PDF report from your extraction data. Ideal for sharing with clients or stakeholders. Business and Agency plans add white-label templates.

Available

Webhook / n8n / Make / Zapier

Fire a webhook on extraction complete. Plug DatIQ into any n8n, Make, or Zapier workflow in minutes.

Available

Email Share

Email a link or summary of any extraction directly from the Dashboard. Works without sign-in for the recipient.

Available

Google Sheets

Use "Open in Google Sheets" from any export dropdown for the one-click path to a fresh sheet with the structured data. Pro plan and above.

Available

Public Share Links

Generate a public link at datiq.app/p/<slug> for any extraction. Read-only, no sign-in required, revocable any time.

Available

Airtable

Push extraction rows straight into an Airtable base. API key + base + table → POST records. Field mapping handles contacts, links, and headings automatically. Pro plan and above.

Available

Notion

Export structured extraction data to a Notion database with field mapping. Includes auto-property detection for common schema. Notion integration probes your database for its existing schema before writing. Pro plan and above.

Coming soon

HubSpot

Push contact and company enrichment data directly to HubSpot CRM. Auto-create contacts and enrich existing records. Business and Agency plans.

Coming soon

Salesforce

Sync extracted leads and company data to Salesforce. Map DatIQ fields to custom Salesforce objects. Business and Agency plans.

Coming soon

Slack

Post extraction summaries and change-monitoring alerts to a Slack channel. Use with or without a DatIQ account.

Coming soon

REST API + Webhooks (Pillar 4)

First-class REST API for developers. Webhook push on every extraction. SDK for Python, Node, and Ruby. Developer plan (H3 2026).
