How does DatIQ work?

  1. Paste a URL on the home screen (or up to hundreds of URLs for a batch run).
  2. Choose options — pick intent chips (summary, contacts, pricing, map, custom) or describe a custom field in plain English.
  3. AI extracts the structured data and renders it on the Preview screen.
  4. Enrich the extraction with one-click capabilities (leadership, social links, mission, pricing breakdown).
  5. Save the extraction to your Dashboard, group it under a Collection, and tag it.
  6. Export to CSV or PDF, share via a public link, or push to HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Slack, or Zapier (one-click from Preview, Dashboard, and Batch).

Features

Do I need to write code to use DatIQ?

No. DatIQ is zero-code — paste a URL, describe any field you want in plain English, and the AI extracts it. Custom extraction is configurable in the UI without scripting.

What formats can I export?

CSV (comprehensive — every enrichment included) and PDF (jsPDF report, customizable with white-label templates on Business and Agency plans). Google Sheets export is available on Pro and above. HubSpot and Salesforce integrations are on Business and above.

Can I schedule recurring extractions?

Yes. Scheduled monitoring is included on Pro, Business, and Agency plans. Schedules can run hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly, and you can attach an alert email to be notified when the page changes.

Does DatIQ offer an API?

Yes. The Developer plan (H3 2026) is an API-first tier. Business and Agency plans include API access today — POST to /api/extract, /api/ai, and GET /api/stats.

Can I share an extraction with someone who doesn't have a DatIQ account?

Yes — every extraction has a Share button that creates a public link at datiq.app/p/<short-code>. The recipient sees a read-only report, no sign-in required. You can revoke the link at any time.

How do I export to a spreadsheet?

Use CSV from any export menu, then open it in your spreadsheet tool.

Will scheduled runs email me?

Only if you add an alert email to the schedule. Automated runs that detect a change send the alert; the manual Run now button just shows you the result on screen.

How do I get to a specific page fast?

Press mod+k (or ctrl+k) anywhere to open the command palette. Type a page name (Home, Dashboard, Pricing, Schedules) and press Enter.

What are those chips above the URL box?

Those are outcome tiles — pre-wired shortcuts for the six most common jobs (AI summary, lead list, pricing, competitor research, job board, custom). Click one to pre-fill the URL, intent and prompt. Click several to combine prompts into one extraction.

What is the Workspace page?

When you sign in, the top nav gains a Workspace entry — a logged-in command center for your recent extractions, schedules, batch runs and notifications.

What is the Gallery?

/gallery lists recent public extractions. Useful for browsing what others have shared and for discovering new use-cases.

Troubleshooting

My extraction came back thin or empty — what should I do?

Some pages block automated access or load their content with heavy JavaScript. Try enabling JavaScript rendering in Advanced options, or try a more specific page (e.g. the pricing page rather than the home page).

The AI summary looks generic — why?

Summaries reflect what the page actually exposes. Pages with little text, or that hide content behind logins, give the model less to work with. If a page is sparse, try a richer sub-page like /about or /pricing.

A batch URL failed. How do I find out why?

The results table shows the reason next to each failed URL. Common causes are unreachable sites, non-HTML pages (e.g. PDFs, images), or sites that block automated requests. Other URLs in the batch still succeed.

"App updated — please refresh."

DatIQ ships improvements regularly. If you've had a tab open for a while and an export or action shows this message, just refresh the page and try again.

Account & data

Where is my data stored?

All extractions and account data are stored in Supabase (Postgres) on encrypted-at-rest infrastructure in the US-East region. DatIQ is DPDP Act 2023 compliant and never trains on your extractions. See the Privacy Policy for the full data handling notice.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Account → Danger zone → Delete account. Account deletion removes all extractions, schedules, collections, and account data within 30 days. Billing records are retained for the legally required minimum (typically 8 years for Indian tax law).
