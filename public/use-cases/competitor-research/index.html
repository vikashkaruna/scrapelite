Why DatIQ for competitor research

Traditional competitive intelligence is a quarterly project: someone Googles the top 5 competitors, bookmarks their pages, and writes a 30-slide deck. Six weeks later, half the data is already stale. DatIQ turns it into a continuous feed.

Step-by-step: monitor a competitor in 4 steps

Step 1

Pick the URLs to watch

Identify 3-10 high-signal pages per competitor: pricing, product, about, blog, careers. Paste each one into DatIQ and save the extraction.

Step 2

Add a Schedule

Open the Schedule tab on any saved extraction. Pick hourly / daily / weekly / monthly. DatIQ re-runs the extraction on cadence and persists each snapshot.

Step 3

Set a change alert

Attach an alert email to the schedule. DatIQ diffs each run against the previous and emails a summary only when something changed. No noise.

Step 4

Build battle cards

When you need a side-by-side, paste two competitor URLs into Compare. DatIQ returns a battle card you can share with sales, product, or execs.

Tips from the field

Tip 1. Don't monitor a competitor's entire site — pick the 3-10 pages that map to your strategic questions (pricing, key feature, leadership, hiring). Less is more for change detection.
Tip 2. For pricing pages, use the custom extraction prompt: "Extract every plan with name, price, billing cadence, and a 1-line description of the most important feature on that tier." This is more useful than the default pricing schema for comparison.
Tip 3. When the alert email fires, open the DatIQ dashboard. The diff view shows what changed and which field. Read the AI summary — it usually explains "why this change might matter."
Tip 4. Run a monthly competitor sweep. Use the domain map to discover new pages on the competitor's site (new product launches, new positioning pages), then add them to your tracking set.

Common questions

How does DatIQ track competitor pricing changes?

Set up a Schedule (Pro+) on any competitor's pricing or product page. Choose hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly. DatIQ re-extracts the page, diffs the pricing tiers, and emails you when something changed.

Can DatIQ compare two competitors side by side?

Yes — the Compare content format takes two URLs and returns a battle card with pricing, features, target audience, and a strengths/weaknesses table. The /vs/battlecard static page generates a shareable card on demand.

How many competitors can I track?

No hard cap on URLs. The limit is your monthly extraction budget. A daily check of 10 competitors costs 300 extractions/month — well within Business.

Can I see when a competitor changes their homepage messaging?

Yes — schedule the homepage with a custom prompt: "Extract the H1, sub-headline, and three primary CTAs." DatIQ highlights changes against the previous run.
