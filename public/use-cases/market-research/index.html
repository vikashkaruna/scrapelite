Why DatIQ for market research

Market research has always been an exercise in patience — analyst time, manual lookups, stale spreadsheets. DatIQ turns the data-collection step into a 20-minute batch job, leaving the actual analysis (the part that needs a human) for the analyst.

Domain mapping for full coverage

Toggle "Map entire domain" on any company URL. DatIQ returns every crawlable URL on the site — the full market surface area for that company.

Batch up to 500 URLs

Drop a CSV of company URLs into the Batch composer. DatIQ extracts each one, with per-row status (success / fail / retry) and a single CSV export. Pro and above plans.

Custom schema for your market

Use the custom extraction prompt to capture the fields your market cares about — e.g. "For every SaaS company, extract pricing model, deployment type, target customer size, and primary GTM motion."

Scheduled quarterly sweeps

Set up a Schedule (Pro+) on a curated list of company URLs. Re-run quarterly to catch pricing changes, leadership moves, and product launches between sweeps.

Aggregate review sentiment

Paste a G2 or Capterra product page. DatIQ extracts every review summary, rating, and date. For hundreds of reviews, use a custom prompt to capture the full text.

JSON to your warehouse

The /api/extract endpoint returns structured JSON. Wire it to Snowflake / BigQuery via a scheduled Netlify Function and you have a continuously-updated market dataset.

Step-by-step: a quarterly market sweep in 4 steps

Step 1

Build the URL list

Curate 50-500 company URLs in your market. Source: your CRM, a previous sweep, or a market map you maintain. Drop the list into the Batch composer.

Step 2

Write a custom prompt

Tell DatIQ what to extract. Example: "Extract company name, mission, pricing tiers, leadership team, primary product, target customer." One prompt for the whole batch.

Step 3

Run the batch

DatIQ processes the batch in the background. Each row gets a status, a per-URL extraction, and a unified CSV export when done. Estimated time: ~30 seconds per URL.

Step 4

Schedule the next sweep

Save the batch as a template and add a Schedule (Pro+). Quarterly is the default — DatIQ emails you when the next run is done, with a CSV of changes since last time.

Tips from the field

Tip 1. Start with 20 URLs, validate the extraction schema, then scale to 200. The shape of the output (column names, value types) is fixed by your custom prompt — get it right before scaling.
Tip 2. For review aggregation, paste the G2 product page directly. DatIQ's default extraction captures the first 10-20 reviews on the page; for the full set, use a custom prompt with explicit "every review on the page".
Tip 3. Schedule a quarterly sweep with a "diff" alert. The email only fires when something changed since last quarter — pricing, leadership, or a new product page. Less noise than a full re-extraction.
Tip 4. For very large markets (1,000+ companies), split the batch into vertical slices (Fintech, SaaS, E-commerce) and run each separately. Easier to track failures and to re-run just the slice that needs a refresh.

Common questions

How do I do market research with DatIQ?

Build a list of 50-500 company URLs, use the Batch feature with a custom extraction prompt, and DatIQ returns structured JSON for each. Export as CSV or pipe via the API to your data warehouse.

How do I aggregate reviews from G2, Capterra, or App Store?

Paste the product page URL. Default extraction captures the first 10-20 reviews. For the full set, use a custom prompt with explicit "every review on the page".

Can DatIQ pull data into a spreadsheet or data warehouse?

Yes — CSV (every plan), Google Sheets (Pro+), and /api/extract JSON which can be piped to Snowflake / BigQuery via a scheduled Netlify Function. Webhook delivery on Business and Agency.

How often should I re-run a market sweep?

Quarterly is the default. Set up a Schedule on each company's homepage or pricing page with monthly cadence to catch material changes between sweeps.
