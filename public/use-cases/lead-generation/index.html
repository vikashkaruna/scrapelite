Step-by-step: qualify a prospect in 4 steps

Step 1

Paste the company URL

Open DatIQ, drop the prospect's homepage or about page into the composer. Render JavaScript is on by default, so React/Next.js sites work without setup.

Step 2

Toggle Contacts & emails

Or hit the Quick Enrichment button on the preview. The AI auto-populates a leadership-contact prompt and surfaces names, titles, emails, and socials.

Step 3

Review the AI brief

DatIQ returns a 2-3 sentence company summary, the leadership list, a mission statement, and any custom fields you asked for. Verify in 10 seconds.

Step 4

Save + export or push

Save the extraction. Export as CSV, open in Google Sheets, or push directly to HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Slack, or Zapier. The record is now a searchable, reusable prospect.

Tips from the field

Tip 1. For best results, paste the /about or /team page directly — most companies put their leadership list there. If the homepage shows it (a hero carousel, for example), the home URL works fine.
Tip 2. Use the custom extraction prompt: "Find every executive listed on this page, with title, and any contact email. If no email, return LinkedIn URL." This works better than the default contacts prompt for sparse pages.
Tip 3. If a company uses a press contact email (press@company.com) for the leadership section, DatIQ returns that — you can still capture it in your CRM and let sales route appropriately.
Tip 4. Set up a recurring schedule (Pro+) to monitor a prospect's leadership page. When titles change (a new CMO, a departed VP), you get an email and the dashboard updates.

Common questions

How do I extract a lead from a company website?

Paste the URL into DatIQ, enable the "Contacts & emails" toggle (or use Quick Enrichment on the preview), and the AI returns leadership names, titles, emails, and social profiles. Export as CSV or push to HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Slack, or Zapier.

Can DatIQ find personal emails for individual prospects?

DatIQ surfaces only what a company publishes publicly. For personal email finding beyond published contacts, pair DatIQ with a dedicated tool like Hunter or Apollo.
