V1.0+ — 2026-08-08

AEO/GEO/SEO hardening release

Made the site fully discoverable by AI crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot, Claude-User) and shipped canonical /what-is-datiq and /faq answer pages with FAQPage and Article JSON-LD. Pre-rendered 10 SEO marketing routes as static HTML so non-JS crawlers see real content.

  • robots.txt: OAI-SearchBot, Googlebot, Claude-User, Google-Extended, Bytespider all explicitly allowed
  • New /what-is-datiq page with 8-Q&A FAQPage + Article JSON-LD
  • New /faq page with 12-Q&A FAQPage JSON-LD
  • 10 SEO marketing routes converted from SPA-only to static HTML
  • Per-page useSeo hook on the React app pages
  • Google-Extended enabled for Gemini citation
  • Bytespider enabled for ByteDance crawler discoverability

V1.0 — 2026-07

Initial V1.0 release · "Web Intelligence Core"

The first stable release. Built on Pillar 0 (Web Intelligence Core) — the proven single, batch, and scheduled URL-extraction engine that the rest of the platform is built on.

What's in V1.0

Extraction & enrichment

  • Single-URL extraction with headings, links, metadata, and structured content
  • AI summary (provider-agnostic chain: Gemini → Claude → OpenAI)
  • Custom extraction — ask for any field in plain English
  • 5 intent chips: AI summary, Find contacts, Scrape pricing, Map site, Custom
  • 12 built-in templates (startup pricing pages, leadership contacts, job listings, etc.)
  • Background enrichment tabs on /preview (re-run, persist, multi-capability)
  • Per-field provenance (each value tagged with its source span and confidence)
  • Generate content: SEO blog outline, competitor summary, social posts
  • Multi-provider scrape chain (Firecrawl → Spider.cloud → Jina AI → Direct fetch)
  • Headless / render-JS mode (opt-in advanced option)

Distribution & workflow

  • CSV / PDF / JSON / Markdown export of every extraction
  • White-label PDF template (Business + Agency plans)
  • Public share links at datiq.app/p/<slug> (no sign-in required)
  • Email export with PDF attachment
  • Google Sheets "Open in Sheets" one-click path
  • Webhook push on extraction complete (n8n, Make, Zapier)

Batch & scheduling

  • Batch mode: paste up to hundreds of URLs (plan-dependent cap)
  • Domain mapping: discover every indexed URL on a site
  • Scheduled monitoring: hourly, daily, weekly, or custom cadence (Pro+)
  • Change detection with diff preview emailed to you
  • Scheduled-pause-on-lapse, resume-on-renew billing flow

Account & billing

  • 8 plans: Free, Go, Select, Pro, Business, Agency, Developer (H3 2026), Enterprise
  • USD + INR pricing; Razorpay + UPI for India, Stripe for the rest
  • Annual billing (20% off) on every paid plan
  • Numbered, itemized invoices and receipts for every payment (PDF emailed automatically)
  • Re-send or download any past invoice from Account → Invoices
  • Coupon support and referral program (UI on /pricing)

Personas & onboarding

  • 6 personas: Sales, SEO, CI, Market research, Recruiter, Founder
  • App adapts its examples, quick actions, and AI prompts to your persona
  • Onboarding tour with persona selection as the first step
  • Command palette (mod+K) for power users
  • Keyboard shortcuts (g d, g b, g s, g p, /, ?) for navigation

Compliance & data

  • DPDP Act 2023 compliant (data stored in Supabase, US-East, encrypted at rest)
  • Google sign-in, Microsoft sign-in, GitHub sign-in, email + password
  • Account deletion (full data removal within 30 days, billing records retained 8 years per Indian law)
  • Public Privacy Policy and Terms of Service (Bengaluru arbitration seat)
  • Coordinated vulnerability disclosure at security@datiq.app
