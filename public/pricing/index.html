Common questions

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes — upgrades take effect immediately and you only pay the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens if I exceed my monthly extraction limit?

Extractions are paused until your next billing cycle. You can buy an extractions bundle at any time from Account → Usage without upgrading your plan.

Do unused extractions roll over?

No. Monthly extractions reset on your billing date. Annual plans get the full year's quota upfront; monthly plans reset every 30 days.

How does the 20% annual discount work?

Pay for 12 months upfront and the per-month rate drops by 20%. Annual prices are shown next to each plan. You can cancel within 14 days for a full refund on the annual portion.

What's included in the free trial credit?

New Free accounts get 25 extractions on top of the regular 10/month quota. The trial credit is one-time per account and is used before your monthly quota.

What payment methods do you support?

Razorpay + UPI / cards / netbanking / wallets for India. Stripe (cards) for everywhere else. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice / wire transfer.
